ERC-8004 registries (Identity, Reputation, third proxy)

A key exists

Three upgradeable (UUPS) proxies on six chains share one owner: a plain address that signed the upgrades itself. No ownership transfer since deployment. Project docs recommend a multisig or timelock for production. Question sent to the team; awaiting reply.

USDC on Base (token under x402 payments)

A key exists

Proxy admin and owner are plain addresses (no code). The address that can freeze (blacklist) accounts is a plain address that is actively used. The pauser (can halt all transfers) is also a plain address; on Base it has only received 0.5 ETH (2023) and has never sent a transaction. The master minter is a contract. Freezing and pausing are standard issuer compliance functions; roles other than these not checked.
